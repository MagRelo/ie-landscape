## Papers
- **Ma, Xin, Li, Zheng & Deng, *Profit Reallocation Mechanisms in Tree-based Data Trading*, arXiv:2609.33506** (v1 Sep 27, v2 Sep 29). Any budget-feasible reallocation of downstream revenue to ancestors weakly expands trade; budget-balanced ones weakly improve welfare; gains grow with depth and branching. Formal backing for paying upstream from downstream value. Caveats: resale not recruitment; no Sybil model.
