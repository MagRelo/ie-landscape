# Weekly update: 2026-10-12

No new papers this week. Two live programs changed how they pay for referrals, plus two infrastructure updates.

## Competitors / signals
- **Pump.fun Callout Rewards**: from the Oct 10 payout, rewards follow whether followers who acted on a callout profited, not volume; low-cap callouts paid less; methodology undisclosed. Program launched ~Aug 2026 with ~$15M budget. [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/pump-fun-callout-rewards-follower-profits/) → [recent-signals](../competitors/recent-signals.md)
- **SayGM** (Bittensor SN28): affiliate pool split pro rata by referred revenue, capped $1 per $1 paid, 30-day window, computed by a public contract. Launched Oct 6. [SayGM blog](https://saygm.com/blog/get-paid-to-refer-developers-to-saygm-ai-gateway) → [recent-signals](../competitors/recent-signals.md)
