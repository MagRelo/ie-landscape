# Weekly update: 2026-11-09

Binance launched a 7-level, 50-person-capped Sub-Affiliate program with team-wide downside, Circle Research published the first Sybil-resistant reputation system built on real x402 settlement data, and a new paper argues Sybil defenses must charge for keeping identities active, not just creating them.

## Papers
- **Ramji, Vander Vos, Zeng & Le (Circle Research), *Anduin: A Sybil-Resistant Agentic Marketplace Reputation System*, arXiv:2611.05586 (v1 Nov 3)**: a provider's rating comes from flow paths starting at providers the buyer already pays, over a 157-day x402 dataset (66.07M payments, 16 chains, $6.1M volume); manipulation costs 20% of the targeted buyers' spend, ~23x harder than collaborative filtering (authors' figures). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05586) → [papers/sybil-resistance](../papers/sybil-resistance.md)
- **Maleki, Sainz, Legarda & Santos-Grueiro, *Beyond Identity Creation: Bilinear Sybil Cost in Identities and Time*, arXiv:2611.06268 (v1 Nov 4)**: defines "bilinear" Sybil cost (s identities for T periods cost ≥ sT/A), gives four independent sufficient conditions, and shows an identity-bound VDF meets them with A of 2–8 on CPUs. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06268) → [papers/sybil-resistance](../papers/sybil-resistance.md)

## Competitors / signals
- **Binance Sub-Affiliate** (announced 2026-11-09): up to 7 levels, at most 50 Sub-Affiliates per Top Affiliate, each level sets its invitees' rates (≥20% passed down, ≥5% kept at setup), team total capped at the Top Affiliate's rate, commissions can be zeroed when another team member is restricted for compliance and clawed back for breaches; KYC required. [Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/9a4535f2f65149f1a09932fbefa11c7a) → [competitors/recent-signals](../competitors/recent-signals.md)

## Product
- Design hooks: (1) the per-code child cap now has a large live comparable (Binance: 50 per team, 7 levels), and Binance's team cap is on total team size, not direct children, which is worth deciding explicitly for issue #12; (2) Binance's "team payout ≤ top rate" is the same conserved-budget idea as renormalizing weights under Option D, but referralTree publishes the rule and the sink for the unclaimed remainder instead of leaving it to discretion; (3) score sub-trees by settled value flowing in from trusted nodes (Anduin), not registrations; (4) add a per-period activity requirement so dormant depth-10 chains pay to stay (bilinear Sybil cost).
