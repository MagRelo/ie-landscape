# Weekly update: 2026-11-02

x402 can now split one agent payment atomically across up to 8 extra recipients (spec only), a NeurIPS paper gives a formal frame for order-dependent rewards (the early-mover premium), and two items missed earlier (FTC's Amway order and BULK's invite-slot referral program) give concrete precedents for upline haircuts and capped codes.

## Papers
- **Goyal, Doshi & Nath, *Sequential Solution Concepts in Cooperative Games with Generalized Characteristic Functions*, arXiv:2510.11255 (v5 Oct 25; NeurIPS 2026)**: when a coalition's worth depends on arrival order, reward rules satisfying arrival incentives (I4OA), "later arrivals never hurt existing agents" (OIR) and full payout (SE) form a characterized class that is disjoint from every Shapley analog. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11255) → [papers/attribution-shapley](../papers/attribution-shapley.md)
- **Zhang, Li, Shi, Zhang, Xia & Han, *Sybil-Proofness of Generalized Adaptive Clinching and Implications for Blockchain Mechanism Design*, arXiv:2610.36629 (v1 Oct 27)**: splitting into fake identities gains nothing as long as the identities' combined budget is capped by an enforceable true budget; companion arXiv:2610.36635 shows the impossibility once Sybils can also over-report budgets. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36629) → [papers/sybil-resistance](../papers/sybil-resistance.md)
- **Feng, Duan, Xu & Qin, *Optimizing e-referral reward programs in the presence of uncertain conversion rate*, HSSC (Oct 31)**: single-level model; paying referral rewards is optimal only when referrers' psychological cost of a failed recommendation is moderate. Minor. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-09551-4) → [papers/incentive-trees-mlm](../papers/incentive-trees-mlm.md)

## Legal
- **FTC & Washington v. Amway (W.D. Wash. 2:26-cv-03474)**, stipulated order entered Oct 13 per Npros (missed by earlier digests): $225M; for ten years, starting nine months after entry, upline credit is cut by the same proportion as a downline's when real customer sales fall below 70% of volume; no upline credit on a recruit's unsold purchases in their first six months; no enrolling recruits before selling to three real customers. [order PDF](https://search.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/Amway-StipulatedOrder.pdf), [Npros](https://npros.com/news/judge-enters-amway-s-225-million-ftc-settlement-starting-clock-on-payments-and-ibo-notices/) → [papers/mlm-pyramid-legal](../papers/mlm-pyramid-legal.md)
