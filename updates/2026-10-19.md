# Weekly update: 2026-10-19

Two Sybil papers give formal backing to the referrer-liability ladder; competitor moves were single-level and incremental.

## Papers
- **Zhang, Luo & Kate, *AgentValve: Sybil-Resilient Payment Authorization for Permissionless AI Markets*, arXiv:2610.12948 (v1 Oct 9)**: reputation as finite capacity on a trust graph, reserved atomically on-chain per payment; an honest agent's exposure is bounded by the capacity it extends to the adversarial region, regardless of how many Sybils sit there. Bounded with up to 10,000 synthetic Sybils; ~$0.04–$0.21 per common payment (authors' figures). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12948) → [papers/sybil-resistance](../papers/sybil-resistance.md)
- **Javarone, Leonardos & Ventre, *NFT-Based Reward Mechanisms: Sybil Farming, Vesting, and Stochastic Verification*, arXiv:2609.13064 (v1 Sep 11; missed by earlier digests)**: with vesting, random audits and whole-cluster forfeiture, farm rewards grow linearly while escape odds fall geometrically; gives a deterrence frontier in audit cost, vesting and penalty, and shows vesting alone isn't optimal when audits are cheap. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13064) → [papers/sybil-resistance](../papers/sybil-resistance.md)
