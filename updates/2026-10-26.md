# Weekly update: 2026-10-26

A live 15-level multilevel referral program turned up (Orderly), plus two papers: one frames "reward whoever was first" as a Shapley rule, the other ties deterrence to identity-reset cost.

## Papers
- **Mobius et al., *Paying for Information, Not for Clicks: A Block-Level Publisher Content Marketplace for AI Answers*, arXiv:2610.30324 (v1 Oct 21)**: equal-share, first-publisher and indispensability payment rules as Shapley variants; on 1,974 queries they only diverge with a global index of who published what and when (premium publishers 42% under equal sharing vs 46–50% under first-publication, authors' figures). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30324) → [papers/attribution-shapley](../papers/attribution-shapley.md)
- **Gatta, Naviglio & Tarantelli, *Tempting the Agent: The Economics of Reputation without Persistent Identity in AI Agent Markets*, arXiv:2609.02992 (v2 Oct 21)**: when identities are cheap to recreate, agents cash out reputation and restart; temptation depends on reset cost, persistence and enforcement. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02992) → [papers/sybil-resistance](../papers/sybil-resistance.md)
