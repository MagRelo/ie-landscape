# Weekly update: 2026-11-23

Quiet week for referral mechanism papers; the useful finds are a measurement study showing AI-assistant referrals mostly arrive with no trace of the referring link, two independent x402 usage reads showing the real money moving is small ($274K settled in all of October 2026; $118.5K to directory-listed sellers on Base over 70 days), and (backfill) a Sybil paper showing per-account verification costs overstate what a farm actually pays.

## Papers
- **Iannelli & Ai, *Straight to the Product: What Online Retailers Miss When Shoppers Ask AI First*, arXiv:2611.20378 (v1 Nov 16)**: linking assistant conversations to later browsing at twenty large retailers, visits to the named product within a day rise from 0.02 to 2.07 per 100 answers among shoppers new to the store that week, but 89% of those visits carry no sign of the assistant's link and 54% follow a web search (authors' figures). [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20378) → [papers/attribution-shapley](../papers/attribution-shapley.md)
- **Maleki, Sainz, Legarda & Santos-Grueiro, *Identity Amortization: Shared Resources and the Cost of Sybil Attacks*, arXiv:2610.32685 (v1 Oct 23; backfill)**: when one resource (phone number, IP, deposit) can back several identities, the per-identity Sybil price is overstated; exact tests for onset and size, and a sharing cap below onset removes it. Per-account phone-verification estimates overstate real cost 1.6–4.3x (authors' figures). Same group as last fortnight's bilinear-cost paper; their *Bounded Participation Channel* (arXiv:2609.35300, Sep 28) is noted alongside. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32685) → [papers/sybil-resistance](../papers/sybil-resistance.md)

## Product
- Design hooks: (1) agent-driven referrals mostly don't come through links (Iannelli & Ai), so for agent work the evidence for `rewardRoots` should be carried in the payment or signed action (ERC-8021 suffix, x402 referral token), and link-based numbers should be described as a floor; (2) budget per-node Sybil cost as (verification price ÷ accounts one resource can back) and cap reuse of a verified resource to one node per `groupId` (identity amortization); (3) size the agent channel honestly: all of x402 settled $274K in October 2026 and directory-listed sellers on Base received $118.5K over 70 days, so referralTree's agent-side payouts will be small for now and the case rests on human-referral groups like Play the Cut.
