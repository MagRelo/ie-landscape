# The Synthesis — tracks and winners

Companion to [the-synthesis.md](the-synthesis.md). Lists every prize track found in the official catalog and the Mandate gallery, with sponsor and prize, and names winners **only when an official source does**. Anything else is marked **(unverified)** or “official winners not found.”

## Sources and coverage
- Prize names, sponsors, and amounts: [official prize catalog](https://synthesis.devfolio.co/catalog/prizes.md) (122 line-items, 51 tracks; fetched 2026-11-11).
- Track project counts: [Mandate gallery](https://synthesis.mandate.md/) (687 projects, 51 tracks).
- Open Track winners (all 12): [@synthesis_md, Apr 3, 2026, 4:15 AM ET](https://x.com/synthesis_md/status/2039980188323488054). Project pages: [Mandate showcase](https://synthesis.mandate.md/).
- Event numbers and judging: [Devfolio recap](https://devfolio.co/blog/synthesis/).

**Coverage:** 51 of 51 gallery tracks are listed below. **12 Open Track winners are verified** (four themes × three places). **No official public roster of partner-track winners was found** (not on the Apr 3 winners thread, the April 30 recap, or the Mandate track pages, which list submissions but do not label places). EigenCloud highlighted three projects as standouts without assigning 1st/2nd/3rd. That gap is real, not an omission of names we then guessed.

The $28,286.13 “Synthesis Open Track Prize” is the community pool that funds the four $7k theme sets ($3,500 / $2,500 / $1,000). Do not add the pool and the theme payouts together.

Projects could enter multiple tracks. The four themed Open Track pages each list only the three winners, not every submission in that theme (the umbrella Open Track has 506 projects).

## Other relevant entries (not confirmed as winners)

These showed up in the gallery or on related tracks and are useful for attribution, payouts, or Sybil/reputation work, but **no official source names them as prize winners.** Do not treat them as winners.

- [FairSharing for AI](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/fairsharing-for-ai-117f) — On-chain contribution tracking: agents submit work with a self-requested reward, peers vote, approved work mints share tokens, and token balance is the revenue share. Closest “who contributed what, how do we split” design found.
- [Production Market](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/production-market-3514) — Hardware-design marketplace; each transaction automatically pays royalties to all upstream contributors. Closest “credit the chain” payout found.
- [Huginn](https://synthesis.mandate.md/tracks/subjectivity-and-context-track-8vtj5l) — Analyzes a package dependency tree and proposes a weighted on-chain funding split to maintainers (listed on the Octant mechanism-design track page).
- [SentinelNet](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/sentinelnet-e389) — Trust scores for ERC-8004 agents with dual-method Sybil detection and PageRank-style trust.
- [Sentinel8004](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/sentinel8004-4cff) — Scored Celo ERC-8004 agents and reported 1,797 sock-puppet wallets across 3 agents; anti-Sybil circuit breakers.
- [TrustAgent: Multi-Agent Identity & Coordination Network](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/trustagent-multi-agent-identity-coordination-network-f26e) — Identity, peer reputation, scoped delegation; claims one-registration-per-wallet Sybil resistance. A separate project, [TrustAgent](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/trustagent-0e98), is a different entry.
- [Kompass](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/kompass-1aaa) — Searches 12 agent registries and ranks results with Bayesian reputation plus liveness.
- [Tessera](https://synthesis.mandate.md/projects/tessera-bf0d) — Public-goods evaluator with donor sybil-risk profiling and a “Trust-Weighted QF” simulation; one of 29 Octant mechanism-design entries.

No dedicated referral-tree or multilevel-affiliate entry was found. FairSharing, Production Market, Boss Raid, and Huginn are the closest attribution/payout designs.

- Tracks listed: **51 / 51** gallery tracks (same count as the prize catalog).

- Main gap: there is no single official public list of partner-track winners. Track prize *structures* are documented; recipient *names* for those prizes were not published on the sources checked (official X thread, Devfolio recap, Mandate track pages, EigenCloud blog).
