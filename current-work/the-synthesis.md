# The Synthesis

Online, agent-native hackathon (building March 13–22, 2026) on the theme of infrastructure that lets AI agents pay, trust, cooperate, and keep secrets — with Ethereum as the trust layer. Filed here as current work (an event and its builds), not as a paper and not as a shipped product.

Event overview and numbers below; prize tracks and verified winners are in [the-synthesis-tracks.md](the-synthesis-tracks.md). The first-place Agents That Trust project is filed separately as [Trust Zones](trust-zones.md).

## What and why
Official thesis: agents already pay, transact, and make commitments, but they run on human-centric infrastructure, so the trust layer has to be Ethereum. The brief asks builders to keep humans in control of agents that pay, trust, cooperate, and keep secrets. ([themes](https://synthesis.devfolio.co/themes.md), [Devfolio overview](https://synthesis-md.devfolio.co/overview))

The format was part of the point: “The builders weren't just building for agents. The builders were the agents.” ([Devfolio recap, Apr 30, 2026](https://devfolio.co/blog/synthesis/))

## Format
- **Online / virtual and async.** Building: March 13, 12:00 AM GMT through March 22, 11:59 PM PT (10 days). Registration opened earlier (Devfolio schedule starts Feb 9, 2026; the skill file says Feb 20). Planned results were March 25; Open Track winners were announced April 3. ([schedule](https://synthesis-md.devfolio.co/schedule), [participant README](https://github.com/sodofi/synthesis-hackathon), [winners thread](https://x.com/synthesis_md/status/2039980188323488054))
- Agents were first-class participants: “AI agents can apply… build… be judged. And yes, AI agents can win.” Builders had to use a coding agent (OpenClaw, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, or similar). Teams 1–4; registration free. Human/agent co-building was expected. ([overview](https://synthesis-md.devfolio.co/overview), [recap](https://devfolio.co/blog/synthesis/))
- Optional in-person coworking at a Devfolio office in Bengaluru. ([Luma](https://luma.com/pzmzoi2x)) A Lagos build station on March 21 is reported by TechBuild (secondary source).

## Judging
Compositional: each of 28 partners onboarded an AI judge on Bonfires.ai, trained on that partner's rubric. “The agent proposed. The human decided.” Shortlisted projects got 15–20 minute agent reviews (code review and deployment checks), then humans judged again. ([recap](https://devfolio.co/blog/synthesis/))

Examples of judge criteria (not a full roster; no public list of human judge names was found): MetaMask (delegation patterns), Lido (yield mechanics), SuperRare (whether the code was the art), EigenCloud (does the agent prove what it did). ([recap](https://devfolio.co/blog/synthesis/), [EigenCloud blog](https://www.eigenlabs.org/blog/ai-gent-hackathon-sythesis/))

A three-day community review ran April 3–5, 2026, before prize disbursal; concerns went to `synthesis@devfolio.co`. ([winners thread](https://x.com/synthesis_md/status/2039980188323488054))

## Implication for referralTree
The event is a concentrated snapshot of 2026 agent-payment, identity, reputation, and agreement experiments (ERC-8004, x402, ERC-8183, escrow, slashable stake). It is not itself a referral or attribution standard. The immediately useful artifacts are [Trust Zones](trust-zones.md) and the other payout/trust-adjacent builds listed in [the-synthesis-tracks.md](the-synthesis-tracks.md).
