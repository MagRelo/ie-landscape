# Crypto quest platforms

Campaign platforms that pay users (points, tokens, NFTs) for completing on-chain/social tasks. Adjacent: they own distribution and campaign UX, not multilevel graph credit.

### Layer3
- **What:** quest and incentive-distribution platform for protocols ("orchestration" of on-chain actions). <https://app.layer3.xyz/business>
- **Mechanism:** protocols fund quests; users complete on-chain actions; rewards in tokens/points; referral features are single-level.
- **Traction:** $16.6M revenue in 2024 and a ~$20M run rate, profitable from Q1 2024, per Alea Research's Layer3 thesis (<https://www.alearesearch.io/reports/theses/layer3>) (investor-research source, not audited).
- **vs referralTree:** campaign ops and sybil filtering are their product; referralTree is the attribution/split substrate. Partner, don't compete.

### Galxe
- **What:** credential and quest platform; also runs the Gravity chain. <https://www.galxe.com/>
- **Mechanism:** projects define quests over on-chain/off-chain credentials; users earn points/NFTs/tokens; heavy Sybil filtering is part of the offer.
- **Traction:** 36M users, 7,700+ partner projects and ~1M daily active users, per Galxe's own 2025 Year in Review (<https://www.galxe.com/blog/galxe-2025-year-in-review>) (self-reported).
- **vs referralTree:** huge distribution, but credit is per-task, not path-based. A natural source of verified "qualifying actions" that could trigger `rewardRoots`.
