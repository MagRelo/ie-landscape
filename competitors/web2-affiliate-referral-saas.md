# Web2 affiliate and referral SaaS

Hosted tracking, partner management and payouts. They solve growth ops and fraud review as a service; attribution is off-chain and usually single-level.

| Name | What it is | Mechanism | Traction (public, sourced) | vs referralTree |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Impact.com** (<https://impact.com>) | Partnership management platform (affiliates, influencers, B2B partners) | Tracking links/pixels, contracts per partner, payouts via platform | No figures verified this pass | Enterprise ops suite; opaque, custodial payouts; single-hop credit |
| **PartnerStack** (<https://partnerstack.com>) | B2B SaaS partner/affiliate/referral platform | Partner tiers, tracked links, commissions paid by platform | ~$35–40M raised and acquisition by AppDirect (Apr 2026) per PitchBook/CB Insights summaries in earlier brief **(unverified)** | Same as above; B2B focus |
| **Rewardful / FirstPromoter / ReferralCandy / Tolt** (e.g., <https://www.rewardful.com>) | Self-serve affiliate/referral tooling for SaaS and e-commerce, usually Stripe/Shopify-integrated | Coupon or link attribution, % recurring commission, some support a 2nd tier | No figures verified | Cheap and easy, but trust-us accounting; no public graph |

**Mechanism pattern:** last-touch link/cookie attribution → commission rule → platform-run payout after a hold. Fraud handling is manual review plus heuristics.

**Where referralTree differs:** public, unrewritable edges and payout receipts (verification/enforcement friction falls); multilevel geometric credit; no custodial balance. Where they win: CRM, partner recruitment, tax/KYC, support.
